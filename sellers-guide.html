Step 1 of 5

Create your account

Starting your online store does not have to be complicated. Create your Vendorly account and take the first step towards putting your business online.

What to do

  • Sign up using your email address.
  • Enter the details needed to create your account.
  • Log in and get ready to set up your store.

You do not need to have everything figured out before you begin. You can start with the basics and build from there.

Back to all steps
02
Step 2 of 5

Set up your store

Give your business a proper home online. Your store details help customers understand who you are and what you sell.

What to do

  • Choose a name for your store.
  • Add a short description of your business.
  • Upload your logo or a profile image if you have one.
  • Check that your store details are correct.

Keep it simple and make sure your store reflects what your business is all about.

Back to all steps
03
Step 3 of 5

Add your products

Now it is time to show customers what you have to offer. You do not need to upload your entire catalog before your store can be useful.

What to do

  • Add a product you currently sell.
  • Upload a clear photo.
  • Enter the product name and price.
  • Add a short description.
  • Repeat the process for your other products.

Start with a few of your best products, then add more as your store grows. Remember to keep your prices and product availability up to date.

Back to all steps
04
Step 4 of 5

Share your store link

Your store is ready to be seen. Vendorly gives you one link that you can share with customers wherever you promote your business.

Where to share it

  • Your WhatsApp status.
  • Your Instagram, TikTok, or other social media bio.
  • Relevant WhatsApp groups.
  • Direct messages with interested customers.
  • Your business cards or product packaging.

Instead of sending pictures and prices one by one, customers can visit your store and browse your products themselves.

Back to all steps
05
Step 5 of 5

Receive orders

This is where your store starts helping you with everyday selling. Customers can browse your products, check the details, and contact you when they want to place an order.

What to do

  • Keep an eye on messages from interested customers.
  • Confirm product availability and prices.
  • Agree with the customer on delivery or pickup details.
  • Confirm the order and arrange fulfilment.
  • Update your product information when something sells out.

You can continue using WhatsApp to communicate with customers while Vendorly gives your business a simple online storefront.
