Getting started

Get your store ready to share

You do not need to be a developer or a designer to get started. Just set up the basics, add a few products, and you will have a store link you can share with customers.

  1. Create your account. Sign up with your email or Google no card required.
  2. Set up your store basics. Store name, a short description, and a logo or profile photo if you have one.
  3. Add your first three products. You can add more later don't wait until your whole catalog is ready.
  4. Copy your store link. It's generated automatically the moment your store is created.
  5. Share it once, anywhere. Bio link, status update, group chat the same link works everywhere.

Do not worry about making everything perfect on your first day. Get your store up, add your best products, and start sharing it. You can improve it as you go.

Product presentation

Take product photos customers can trust

You do not need an expensive camera to take good product photos. A clean background, enough light, and a steady phone can make a big difference.

  • Use natural light. Shoot near a window during the day instead of relying on your flash.
  • Keep a plain background. A wall, a table, or a plain cloth keeps the focus on the product.
  • Shoot straight on. Avoid awkward angles that distort the product's shape or size.
  • Show scale where it matters. For jewelry, bags, or accessories, one photo being worn or held helps customers judge size.
  • Stay consistent. Similar lighting and background across products makes your whole store feel put-together.

If you want to create a promotional post quickly, the AI Marketing Studio can help turn a product photo into a flyer you can share.

Pricing

Set prices without second guessing yourself

Your price should cover what you spend and the work you put in. A simple approach can help you set prices without guessing.

  1. Add up your real cost. Materials, packaging, and any delivery cost you absorb not just the item itself.
  2. Factor in your time. If you made it yourself, your time has a value even if it doesn't feel like a "cost."
  3. Check what similar sellers charge. You don't need to be the cheapest you need to be reasonable for the quality you offer.
  4. Round to a clean number. Prices like ₦8,500 feel more intentional than ₦8,432.
  5. Revisit pricing every so often. As costs change, your prices should too don't lock yourself into an old number forever.

Most importantly, know why you chose your price. When you understand your costs and the value you offer, it becomes easier to explain your price to customers.

Getting customers

Get more people to your store

Your store link is most useful when customers can easily find it. Here are a few places worth sharing it:

  • Your social bios. Instagram, TikTok, and X all support a single clickable link make it your store, not a generic homepage.
  • WhatsApp status and group chats. Pin it or repost it regularly so new contacts see it too.
  • Every new customer conversation. Send the link instead of describing products one by one.

Make sharing part of your routine. The easier you make it for people to find your store, the easier it is for new customers to discover what you sell.
